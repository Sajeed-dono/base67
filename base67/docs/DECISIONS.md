## Approved service-policy audit corrections (2026-11-10)

- Owner approved the correction plan and Clock Option A after the independent REJECT audit. This section supersedes the affected historical mechanics. Existing gameplay policies remain GAME VALUE, provisional. No new cost, rate, capacity, duration, multiplier or balance value is introduced.
- A job's immutable repairDamageId identifies damage independently of recoveryId/payment. observeDamage admits a configured occurrence's actual before/after count transition, mints a private immutable observation and receipt, and updates only the unwired progression snapshot. This is admission of an external observation, not simulation event execution. Confirmation requires that record; current positive damage cannot relabel unidentified active or completed repairs. The future mission boundary must supply the actual occurrence when observed; modified aggregates are not authenticated.
- Repairs reserve one unit of a specific observation. Existing unidentified damage remains ordinarily repairable through non-authoritative allocations. Ordinary and assisted completions resolve the same bound unit. Last-unit resolution retains receipts/tombstone, removes unused restricted credit and prevents reopening. Cancellation releases the allocation unchanged; assistance restores fully to its original entitlement and ordinary payments retain 50%/floor rules. Other occurrences on the facility remain independent.
- recordFieldRepair reports an already-executed/paid external outcome for an open, unreserved allocation and matching count decrement. It is not a free repair action; it neither charges a second spare nor runs the engine. Future coordination must combine external payment/outcome and aggregate update atomically. Invalid external damage replacement is logged and cannot silently complete timed work; normal cancellation remains available.
- Installed counts exclude unfinished units. First activation marks lifecycle-only state underConstruction; completion replaces absent/notBuilt/underConstruction with operating (damaged if applicable). Explicit off/outOfResources preferences survive. Expansion preserves existing operation, damage and tier. First cancellation restores unbuilt lifecycle; expansion cancellation leaves installed units untouched. Positive installed counts with lifecycle-only or unknown operating states are rejected rather than guessed. No per-unit switch system is added.
- Clock Option A: ClockObservation bundles UTC and a nonnegative capture-order token. Producers must capture both together before delivery, increase tokens across the aggregate, and never attach a fresh token to stale UTC. start/activate/cancel/reconcile reject token <= lastObservationToken before enqueue, reconciliation, pending delivery, completion or payment. Job clocks also reject old/duplicate tokens. Accepted observations retain elapsed=max(0, UTC-checkpoint), remaining=max(0, priorRemaining-elapsed), checkpoint=UTC even on rollback. Original timestamps/terminal receipts remain unchanged; new jobs use accepted current UTC. No reader, coordinator or CampaignSave fields are added. Fresh observations are consumed even for invalid start commands, preventing an older delayed sample from being accepted afterward. Tokens prove ordering, not elapsed time; accepted forward jumps can still finish early. Producer correctness and future durable token storage remain separately gated integration concerns.
- resources['spare_part'] is the canonical available consumable balance. unpackSpareParts validates finite whole nonnegative stock, moves packed spares into an empty/zero available balance and removes the packed entry atomically. Repetition is a no-op. Mixed positive representations are ambiguous without provenance and rejected unchanged. Repairs require explicit unpacking; spares never enter returnable facility reservations. Ordinary/assisted refunds remain unchanged.

Schema 2, timer helper, fixture, app/prologue/UI/sim, data/checksums, dependencies and balance are preserved. In-memory service fields are not a save schema and imply no restart durability. ### Implemented policy (all gameplay rules GAME VALUE, provisional)

- Recovery is restricted, recipe-bound assistance for mandatory installed-count objectives and confirmed essential repair occurrences. It is not ordinary inventory, cannot fund optional units/upgrades/research, and cannot be converted into an unrestricted refund. The old once-per-level eight-material cap is superseded.
- Construction identity is derived from a known facility and its configured required installed count. A caller identity only selects an existing eligible objective; it cannot define one. Required counts remain one for existing baseline facilities. Extra units are optional unless a later approved objective changes that count.
- Repair authority is an immutable configuration catalogue validated against known campaign levels, facilities, original event damage targets/units and canonical level/event/sequence identities. Confirmation additionally requires matching observed installed damage and an unused occurrence receipt. Recovery validates receipt kind, target, level, amount and remaining damage.
